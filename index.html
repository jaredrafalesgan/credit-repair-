How long does credit repair typically take?

Timelines vary by client because they depend on each individual's credit profile and the number of dispute rounds needed. There's no fixed timeline we can promise — your personalized guidance session will give you a clearer sense of what to expect for your situation.

What is a "dispute round"?

A dispute round is one cycle of reviewing your credit report, identifying items to dispute, and submitting those disputes to the relevant bureaus or furnishers. Some credit profiles resolve in fewer rounds; others may need more, depending on what's found.

What's the difference between personal and business credit help?

Personal credit repair focuses on your individual credit profile. Business credit building focuses on establishing and strengthening credit under your business's name, which is a separate profile from your personal credit.

What do I need to get started?

We'll go over exactly what's needed during your free consultation, since it depends on whether you're pursuing personal credit repair, business credit building, or both. In general, expect to review your current credit reports together.

Can you guarantee results?

No. Results are not guaranteed, and the number of dispute rounds needed depends on each client's individual credit profile and the accuracy and completeness of the information being disputed. We focus on giving you clear, honest guidance rather than promises we can't back up.
